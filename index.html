Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your account

You can request deletion of your KhaoJi account and the data linked to it in either of the two ways below.

Option 1: Request by email

  1. Send an email to khaojiofficial@gmail.com from your registered email, or mention your registered phone number.
  2. Use the subject Account Deletion Request and include your full name and the phone number or email used in the app.
  3. We will verify your identity and delete your account within 7 days. You will get a confirmation reply.
Send deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, phone number, email, profile photo)
  • Your saved addresses and favorites
  • Your login (authentication) record
  • Your device notification token

What may be kept

  • Past order records may be kept for accounting, tax and legal purposes, and for resolving disputes. They are no longer linked to your profile once the account is deleted.
  • Anonymous, non-identifiable usage and crash statistics.
Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. Any active orders should be completed before you request deletion.
